Luce and the Jovians
After his rescue and return to the United States, Luce becomes fascinated by the Jovians, an alien species discovered in Jupiter's atmosphere [1, 2]. He immerses himself in Jovian linguistics, believing he's made progress in deciphering their language [3-6]. Luce manages to secure an interview for a Jovian linguistic research position, but panics when he sees they test for stimulant use, and backs out [7].
Determined to join the research team, Luce tries to get clean and re-enter society. He gets a job as a short-order cook, but continues studying Jovian linguistics [8]. While considering other job options, Luce remains fixated on the Jovians and their language [9].
Realizing a research position may be out of reach, Luce decides to aim for a support role. He applies for various positions in the Jovian system, but none seem suitable [8, 10]. However, a breakthrough occurs when he realizes his knowledge of Spanish can help him understand Portuguese [11]. This newfound skill enables him to secure a job as a cook on the Jovian research platform [11].
Luce’s journey to a position on the Jovian research platform is marked by both setbacks and unexpected opportunities. His passion for Jovian linguistics and his determination to be involved, even in a support role, eventually allow him to achieve his goal.

Luce's Journey to Jupiter
Before embarking on his journey to the Jovian research platform, Luce faces a period of intense preparation driven by his deep fascination with the Jovians and their language. However, his path is not without obstacles, as he must confront personal demons and navigate a changing world.
Here are some key preparations Luce undertakes:
•
Overcoming Stimulant Addiction: Realizing that his stimulant use could hinder his dream of joining the Jovian research team, Luce embarks on a challenging journey to overcome his addiction [1]. He seeks to re-enter society, finds work as a short-order cook, and attempts to manage his dependence [2]. This struggle showcases Luce's determination and the difficulties he faces in breaking free from the clutches of addiction.
•
Deepening His Understanding of Jovian Linguistics: Fueled by an insatiable curiosity, Luce dedicates himself to studying the Jovian language [3, 4]. He spends countless hours transcribing their speech from television broadcasts and meticulously analyzing the patterns and nuances of their communication [5-8]. Despite initial setbacks and misinterpretations [9], Luce perseveres, believing he is on the cusp of unraveling the secrets of their language.
•
Securing Employment on the Jovian Platform: After a failed attempt to secure a research position due to the mandatory drug testing [1], Luce readjusts his strategy and seeks a support role that would grant him access to the Jovian research environment [10, 11]. Recognizing the prevalence of Portuguese speakers among the platform's kitchen staff, he leverages his Spanish language skills to secure a position as a cook [12]. This resourceful approach demonstrates Luce's adaptability and unwavering determination to achieve his goal.
These preparations reveal Luce's resilience, his passion for knowledge, and his willingness to confront personal challenges to pursue his dreams. While the journey to Jupiter is a physical one, it is also a journey of self-discovery and healing for Luce, as he grapples with his past, his addiction, and his evolving understanding of the Jovians.
