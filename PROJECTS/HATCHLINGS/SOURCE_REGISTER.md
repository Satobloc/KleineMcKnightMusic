## Intake rules

- Record original filename and source date when known.
- Never infer a creation date from an upload date.
- Record duplicate files and exact duplicates rather than silently removing them.
- Preserve alternate and conflicted Scrivener states.
- Track any extracted/converted derivative back to its archive path.
- Do not designate a manuscript as current solely because it has the latest date among the files presently available.
