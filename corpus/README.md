# Research Corpus

This directory is reserved for curated, publishable research material derived from the private working corpus.

The original uploaded conversation exports, lyrics, personal writing, and mixed source files are not to be copied here wholesale.

## Separation rule

Keep three layers distinct:

1. **Private originals:** User uploads, conversation exports, original prompt records, lyrics, sketches, and reference lists.
2. **Curated research records:** Extracted observations, normalized terminology, source references, and experiment metadata, with provenance retained.
3. **Public project assets:** Original code, schemas, documentation, and material whose authorship, rights, and publication status have been checked.

## Required metadata

Before adding a corpus item, record:

- source filename or canonical URL;
- author or origin, if known;
- extraction date;
- exact passage or item provenance;
- license or rights status;
- whether the wording is verbatim, normalized, translated, or paraphrased;
- confidence and verification status;
- intended use.

Do not publish copyrighted lyrics, private correspondence, raw ChatGPT exports, or third-party source text without authorization and a rights review.
