ALTERNATE IDEA: Which came first?

 Which Came First? – Trivia Pairs
Jeopardy! (original run)
vs.
The Price Is Right (original run)
→ The Price Is Right (1956 vs. 1964)
Justification: The original Price Is Right with Bill Cullen debuted in 1956. Jeopardy! began in 1964 with Art Fleming.

Alex Trebek hosting Jeopardy!
vs.
Pat Sajak hosting Wheel of Fortune
→ Pat Sajak (1981 vs. 1984)
Justification: Sajak began hosting Wheel of Fortune in 1981. Trebek's Jeopardy! tenure started in the 1984 reboot.

Who Wants to Be a Millionaire (UK debut)
vs.
Who Wants to Be a Millionaire (US debut)
→ UK version (1998 vs. 1999)
Justification: The format originated in the UK in 1998, then launched in the US a year later.

Family Feud
vs.
Wheel of Fortune
→ Family Feud (1976 vs. 1975)
Correct answer: Wheel of Fortune
Justification: Wheel premiered in 1975; Feud followed in 1976.

Deal or No Deal (US version)
vs.
Are You Smarter Than a 5th Grader?
→ Deal or No Deal (2005 vs. 2007)
Justification: Deal premiered on NBC in 2005; 5th Grader came in 2007 on Fox.

The Match Game
vs.
The Newlywed Game
→ Match Game (1962 vs. 1966)
Justification: Match Game started in 1962; Newlywed Game launched four years later.

Drew Carey begins hosting The Price Is Right
vs.
Steve Harvey begins hosting Family Feud
→ Drew Carey (2007 vs. 2010)
Justification: Carey replaced Bob Barker in 2007; Harvey became host of Feud in 2010.

Double Dare
vs.
Nickelodeon GUTS
→ Double Dare (1986 vs. 1992)
Justification: Double Dare was Nickelodeon’s first big game show hit in the '80s; GUTS came later.

Regis Philbin on Millionaire
vs.
Anne Robinson on The Weakest Link (US version)
→ Regis Philbin (1999 vs. 2001)
Justification: Millionaire hit U.S. primetime in 1999; Weakest Link imported in 2001.

Hollywood Squares (original)
vs.
To Tell the Truth
→ To Tell the Truth (1956 vs. 1966)
Justification: To Tell the Truth was part of the early quiz show era, while Hollywood Squares was a mid-’60s launch.
