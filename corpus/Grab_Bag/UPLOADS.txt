Made with hawthorn flowers, almond milk, amydoun, and eggs. SPYNEE: Take and make gode thik Almaund mylke as tofore. and do þerin of flour of hawthorn and make it as a rose & serue it forth. FOR TO MAKE SPINE: Nym the flowrys of the haw thorn clene gaderyd and bray hem al to dust and temper hem wyth Almaunde mylk and aly yt wyth amydoun and wyth eyryn wel þykke and boyle it and messe yt forth and flowrys & levys abovyn on [lain upon it]. [takes its name from Spina of which it is made]
