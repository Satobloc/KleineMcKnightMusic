SUNO TEQs

	1.	COMPACT THREE-FIELD STYLE

[STYLE1:
primary melodic, regional, vocal, or harmonic identity]

[STYLE2:
secondary rhythmic, atmospheric, textural, or genre identity]

[PERCUSSION:
meter, subdivision, grouping, attack, and ensemble behavior]

Example:

[STYLE1:
sultry Vietnamese romantic honky-tonk lounge bandura with slow portamento slurs]

[STYLE2:
shimmering dark Malagasy gospel-step with rolling ooka-chooka pulse]

[PERCUSSION:
rapid nine-subdivision hi-hat grouped 3+2+3 against a heavy three-beat muted slap-banjo cycle; old Trinidadian marching-drum choir in slow ceremonial response]

	2.	COMPOUND STYLE-SENTENCE

Combine:

[texture]
+
[performance behavior]
+
[emotional posture]
+
[genre deformation]
+
[rhythmic or instrumental engine]

Examples:

Spicy half-slap downtrodden capercaillie Sogdian deep stomp.

Twinkly spazz-choppy dank melody, boastful prayer-mumble rap cantina singalong.

Gamelan-a-billy spookenfolky dry-miked wet stabs with hog-thwollop drive.

Thin-blooded, woolgathering folk charm.

Best when every invented phrase implies an audible behavior rather than only mood.

	3.	STRUCTURAL INTERMESH

[STRUCTURAL_INTERMESH:
distinct musical systems occupy the same structure without fully reconciling |
timbral edges remain partly incompatible |
rhythmic cooperation emerges despite harmonic friction |
hooks persist through negotiation between conflicting local rules |
elements adapt around one another without collapsing into stylistic consensus]

Purpose:
Defines how multiple styles coexist across the entire piece.

Use when ordinary “fusion” would sound too smooth.

	4.	CONTACT BEHAVIOR

[CONTACT_BEHAVIOR:
allow timing drag between layers |
preserve regional and acoustic accent signatures |
encourage partial synchronization failures |
permit resonance spillover without full blending |
maintain edge-friction during transitions]

Controls what happens where musical systems touch.

Useful variables:
timing drag |
phase mismatch |
resonance leakage |
unequal phrase lengths |
attack incompatibility |
accent preservation |
partial synchronization |
transition abrasion

	5.	GENRE ENGINE

[GENRE_ENGINE:
measure-level dominance shifts between emotionally direct regional melodic forms and unstable acoustic-material behavior |
neither system fully absorbs the other]

Defines the rule by which styles trade control.

Possible patterns:
measure alternation |
phrase interruption |
foreground/background exchange |
call-and-response control |
gradual contamination |
cyclic overthrow |
one system controls rhythm while another controls melody |
stable hook surrounded by unstable genre

	6.	BREAKDOWN STATE

[BREAKDOWN_STATE:
structural containment weakens |
previously separated behaviors cross-interfere |
hooks survive as shared debris between incompatible systems]

Possible behaviors:
meters leak into one another |
instruments inherit the wrong articulation |
vocal styles swap phrases |
refrains lose words but preserve contour |
cycles continue at incompatible lengths |
harmonic material survives as fragments |
earlier motifs become percussion |
genre boundaries turn temporarily porous

	8.	GLOBAL / SURFACE TWO-LAYER STRUCTURE

[GLOBAL_INTERPRETIVE_LAYER:
non-vocal instructions governing total style, realism, instrumentation, structure, and performance behavior]

[SURFACE_GENERATION_LAYER:
lyrics |
quoted trigger strings |
local vocal entries |
loops |
phase cues |
section changes]

Purpose:
Separates global instructions from material Suno may sing.

Only clearly designated lyric strings should become vocals. Structural language should remain operational rather than performative.

	10.	LOCAL INLINE VOICE CUES

//[FEMALE_VOICE]//

//[WHISPERED_GROUP]//

Use sparingly. Inline cues are less reliable than lyric-trigger syntax and may occasionally be sung aloud or ignored.

	12.	EXCLUSION ARCHITECTURE

-jazz
-bebop
-stage musical
-lounge
-mouth noises
-theater
-oompah
-doo-wop

Purpose:
Stops Suno from resolving unusual instructions into obvious neighboring genres or clichés.

Use exclusions strategically. Block the model’s easiest unwanted interpretation rather than trying to negate every possible bad outcome.

Too many exclusions may weaken the positive identity of the prompt.

	13.	ARRANGEMENT MAP

[INTRO:
ambient field recording and isolated piano]

[VERSE:
dry acoustic figure and restrained lead vocal]

[PRECHORUS:
percussion accumulates without full harmonic lift]

[CHORUS:
bass-forward rhythmic opening with expanded backing voices]

[BRIDGE:

[CLIMAX:

[OUTRO:

Use when experimental material needs a legible song skeleton.

	14.	BEHAVIOR-FIRST INSTRUMENTATION

Instead of:

accordion, guitar, drums

Use:

accordion enters as a slow pressure swell halfway through |
picked guitar repeats a spring-loaded accented tap figure |
drums accelerate without increasing emotional urgency

Describe:

instrument |
attack |
phrase shape |
rhythmic placement |
dynamic behavior |
interaction with other layers |
change across the song

Purpose:
Tells Suno what instruments do rather than merely naming them.

	16.	PERFORMANCE CONTRADICTION

Examples:

dry-miked wet stabs

shimmering dark roll

boastful prayer mumble

sultry ceremonial abrasion

gentle forced yawn

patient panic rhythm

Purpose:
Pairs apparently incompatible descriptors so Suno must negotiate between them.

Best contradictions operate on different dimensions:

dry recording / wet articulation |
dark harmony / bright timbre |
boastful posture / prayer cadence |
gentle dynamics / violent phrasing

	17.	RECURRENCE WITH MUTATION

[HOOK_CONTINUITY:
preserve contour and rhythmic identity while allowing phonetic, harmonic, instrumental, semantic, or stylistic mutation]

Possible sequence:

first appearance:
clean and legible

second:
phonetic corruption

third:
wrong genre

fourth:
instrumental only

fifth:
fragmented but emotionally clarified

Purpose:
Maintains recognizability while allowing severe local instability.

	18.	FAMILIAR-SOURCE DISTORTION

[REFERENCE_FRAME:
establish a recognizable lyric, melodic archetype, cliché, or cultural object]

[MUTATION_RULE:
alter phonetics, morphology, syntax, semantic association, or genre context]

[RECOGNITION_ANCHOR:
retain one or two durable phrases, contours, or placements]

Purpose:
Uses familiar material as an interpretive trellis.

The anchor should not only identify the source. It should make surrounding nonsense retroactively acquire possible meaning.
