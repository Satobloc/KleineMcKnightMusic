PARTS
CHORUS (=REFRAIN / =HOOK)
Memorable, short/abstract summary of story, emotional apex
Music gets bigger (louder, more complex, more instrumentation)

VERSES
Details, more involved
More restrained melodies and accompaniment to keep from distracting from lyrics
Music should change a bit from verse to verse for variety
Slight melody / instrument / backup line changes

BRIDGE
Musically & lyrically different
Introduce new music ideas (melodies, chords, instruments, even keys)
Recontextualize / enrich story
