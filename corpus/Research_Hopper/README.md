# Research Hopper

Intake for peer-reviewed papers, preprints, technical reports, model cards, architecture documentation, datasets and reproducible technical experiments relevant to text-to-audio and music generation.

Capture bibliographic metadata, DOI/arXiv/URL, model/system, architecture, training data claims, conditioning path, evaluation methodology, limitations, code/weight/data licenses separately, and relevance to Suno hypotheses.

Do not treat an architecture from another system as evidence of Suno's private architecture.
