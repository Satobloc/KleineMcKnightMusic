# Community Prompt Patterns — Research Notes 2026-11-10

These are patterns reported by third-party guides and creators. They are not established Suno laws.

## Candidate pattern families
- Layered descriptions: genre/lane, motion, sound sources, lead/vocal, production space.
- Era + genre + mood + instrumentation + production + vocal character.
- Section progression expressed as ordered events or transitions.
- Repeated anchors and explicit local consequences.
- Bracketed section/performance instructions versus plain lyric text.
- Exclude field for unwanted instruments/styles rather than relying only on natural-language negation.
- Cross-domain/persona references as possible association probes.
- Invented language/phonotactic repetition as pronunciation and vocalic probes.
- Rare genre pairings and contradictory descriptors as tests of interaction and model defaults.

## Contradictions to preserve
- Comma-separated descriptors vs structured prose.
- Short prompts vs detailed prompts.
- Early-position weighting vs contextual/local restatement.
- Numeric BPM as a useful cue vs weak/ignored cue.
- Bracket tags as instruction vs occasional leakage into sung text.
- Negative instructions in Style vs dedicated Exclude field.

## Experimental treatment
For each pattern, require exact prompt, input field, current model/version, settings, output references, repeat count, observed result, user interpretation, counterexamples and independent replication status.
