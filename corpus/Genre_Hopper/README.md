# Genre_Hopper

A deliberately low-friction holding area for unsorted material that may be useful to KleineMcKnightMusic.

## Purpose

Drop things here without deciding what they are, where they belong, or how they connect. This is a collection point for later mining, identification, extraction, comparison, tagging, and possible promotion into the project's curated corpus or toolbox.

Examples include:
- genre and tradition references
- terminology, instruments, techniques, rhythmic or formal ideas
- Suno prompts, observations, and experiment notes
- Sonic Pi patterns, templates, and audio-reference ideas
- links, articles, datasets, code, screenshots, audio notes, and odd musical discoveries

## Intake rules

- **No sorting required.** Preserve original filenames and context where practical.
- **Don't silently overwrite.** Add a dated or otherwise distinct filename when a new version is needed.
- **Keep provenance.** When known, retain the source URL, creator, date, license, and a sentence about why the item was collected.
- **Review before promotion.** Items are not vetted, verified, licensed, or approved for reuse merely because they are here.
- **Keep private or restricted material out.** This repository is public. Do not upload private correspondence, raw personal conversation exports, credentials, copyrighted material you are not authorized to redistribute, or confidential files. Link to a private source or keep it in private storage instead.

## Later mining

When asked, inspect the contents in batches and extract useful material into the appropriate curated location. Preserve the original intake item; record interpretations and derived records separately. Distinguish source statements, observed results, hypotheses, and our own inferences.

**Default state: unsorted, unclassified, and waiting to be explored.**
