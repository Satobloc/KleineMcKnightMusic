OPTION 1

ECHO SPIRITS DISTILLING CO WHITE RUM: Flavorful and bold, challenging the mind on what a rum can be with earthy notes of vanilla, molasses, baked pear, and tropical fruits.

PUSSER'S BRITISH NAVY BLUE LABEL ORIGINAL ADMIRALTY RUM: Inspired by the British Admiralty’s blending recipe served in daily rations until 1970. With a full, rounded body, notes of banana and ginger on the nose, and long slightly spicy finish, this clear dark amber rum highlights the flavors of classic demerara sugar, molasses, dried fruits, island spices, and caramel. 

PYRAT XO RESERVE: A delicate amber-hued blend of Caribbean rums aged up to 16 years and expertly crafted for richness and complexity. Featuring a unique burst of aromas that includes orange peel, caramel, honey, and molasses, with a palate that is delicate and smooth, almost sweet, with hints of fresh spices.

PLANTATION PINEAPPLE RUM: Inspired by Charles Dickens' favorite rum. A rich and delicious bouquet, accented by smoky notes and aromas of tropical fruit, pineapple, citrus peel, and a touch of clove. The palate opens with a structured, pleasant sensation of ripe banana, pineapple, and spices layered in a smoky finish.


OPTION 2

451 SPIRITS WRITERS BLOCK: Distilled from Molasses, this unaged flavored rum eschews traditional spices in favor of mint and lime botanical infusions as a dry nod to the Mojito. Moroccan mint tea greets the nose, backed up by hints of dried lime and a bit of heat. The palate follows through on the the nose, blending in hints of white pepper and nougat for a crisp, clean finish.

GOSLINGS BLACK SEAL: Made from a 150-year-old Gosling family recipe, this deep black rum is aged in charred American oak casks. The smooth, full flavor is the result of a careful blending to impart flavor and subtle elegance. The key ingredient in a Dark n Stormy cocktail. Look for caramel, dried fruit & molasses with hints of spice on the nose, followed by molasses, liquorice, vanilla and cinnamon, with herbal notes and a lingering spiced finish touched by brown sugar and cooked herbed fruits.

RON ZACAPA 23 YEAR: A blend of aged rums between 6 and 23 years old usig the solera aging process with barrels from American whiskey, delicate sherries and fine Ximénez wines, creating a smooth & balanced rum. Complex, generous and full-bodied. Starting with a depth of dried fruit and apricot, building to savory oak, nutmeg, leather and tobacco with notes of coffee and vanilla, balanced with a spicy touch of cinnamon and ginger. Finishes with ntricate with honeyed butterscotch, spiced oak and dried fruit. 

PITU CACHACA: Stepping outside traditional rum categories, cachaca is distilled from the juice of fresh sugarcane rather than molassas or other sugar products. This national spirit of Brazil offers an interesting contrast in flavors. Look for classic cachaça aromas reminiscent of fresh cut grass, brine and black olives. You may detect notes of sweet corn and pepper. Starting with a disticnt smokiness, lovers of mezcal may find the flavors reminiscent at first, before the herbal quality builds on the palate.
