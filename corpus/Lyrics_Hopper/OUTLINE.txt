Act I – Into the Sulphur Marsh

What: Hele “Hey” t’Nguir steps over puddles of bioluminescent fungi, inhaling the sulphuric reek.

Style: Three clipped sentences to start—urgent, breathless.

Mood & Theme: clocks ticking; “How much of me is equation?”

Catalyst at Riverside Hotel

Action: Hele slips into the abandoned ballroom-lab, keys achelic cylinder in clay pot’s foam-padded cradle.

Tech & Jargon: “achelic cylinder,” “high-field engines” humming behind sealed doors.

Sensory: steam-hiss from distant reactor coils, sour tang of spent prosthphent on her gloves.

Style Note: “lump-then-leap” — pack exposition into one dense paragraph, then cut to action.

GauBlue’s Rescue

Inciting Incident: He’s inside a cryobox chamber, half-awoken—rats graze on thaw-water.

Conflict: MVA strike-team bursts in; Hele brandishes an improvised stun-rifle.

Pacing: rapid-fire dialogue, single-clause sentences to heighten danger.

Outcome: They fight their way out; GauBlue, dazed, hooks onto her wrist.

First Turning Point – The Note

Revelation: In a dim corner, they find Dr t’Nguir’s encrypted holo-note: “Mutation clocks are real. Trust no equation.”

Hook: Do they run with the data… or chase down his last experiment?

Thematic Beat: identity vs. algorithm; betrayal in every formula.

Act II – Splice, Smuggle, Survive
Under-Spiral Chase

Setting: Steam-flooded maintenance tunnels.

Action: GauBlue’s bioluminescent veins pulse—mutation accelerating.

Style: Short bursts of description, then silence—“leap” out of prose into gunfire.

Tech: “mutation clock,” “ventilator plate,” flickering reactor panels.

Safehouse at Port Pretess

Characters: MVA contact (“Slag”) greets them in a dockside shanty.

Exposition: They smuggle achelic cylinders into a steam-powered barge.

Mood: sour sweat, salt-baked wood, clanging chains.

Style Tip: Alternate longer internal monologue (Hele’s doubts) with staccato external action (hand-to-hand, engine roars).

Midpoint Betrayal

Twist: The Research Coordinator (Gojo Hei) swoops in: “Thanks for playing courier.” She seizes the cylinders and delivers them to a waiting launch rig.

Stakes Spike: GauBlue collapses—his mutation clock is weaponized.

Thematic Punch: Who really owns “equations as DNA”?

Descent to Darkest Hour

Scene: They’re strapped to cryobox frames, countdown on high-field console.

Sensory: electricity arcing, the metallic taste of fear.

Style: One long, unbroken paragraph to trap the reader in time’s freeze.

Act III – Reckoning & Becoming
Hele’s Breakout

Climax: She hacks the high-field engine’s coil matrix—steam hisses turn to geysers.

Action: Leaps into the control core, wrestles a stun-rifle from an MVA guard.

Style: Rapid alternation of 1-word and 20-word sentences to jar the reader.

Saving GauBlue

Moment: She pries open his cryobox just as the mutation clock hits zero.

Revelation: Instead of death, his body glows with stable bioluminescence—a new life form.

Emotional Beat: He reaches for her, voice a soft crackle: “I’m more than equation.”

Escape through the Marsh

Setting: Geyser-spotted canals at dawn.

Action: They commandeer a skiff, MVA gunboats pursue under bridges of fungus glow.

Style: Keep sentences mid-length to sustain suspense without fatiguing.

Final Denouement

Aftermath: In a hidden lab, Hele beams the decrypted gene-equation out to fringe networks.

Image: Fungal spores drifting across a gas-lit sky.

Closing Line: One short, haunting question:

“How much of me was ever just equation?”

Stylistic & Thematic Checklist
“Lump-then-Leap” Exposition: pack sci-fi jargon into dense lumps, then spring into visceral action.

Sentence Rhythm: alternate short (≤5 words) for tension, long (20+ words) for wonder or dread.

Sensory Palette:

Smell: sulphur, spent prosthphent, ozone from coils

Sight: bioluminescent veins, phosphor canals, neon-etched machinery

Sound: steam-hiss, reactor low-thrum, click-whirr of cryobox

Recurring Motifs: mutation clocks, fungal light vs. chemical decay, mirrors (identity).
