DRAMATIS PERSONAE
Cast of Characters: Who's Who, and What's What
Hele "Hey" t'Nguir: The narrator and protagonist. She is trained in field-craft and shown to be quick-thinking and focused under pressure. She has a genuine laugh, is observant, and develops an intimate relationship with GauBlue Ngui. She's kin to the project pilot, Dr t'Nguir and may be involved in a lunar plot. She is also a ceramics specialist, making clay pots, for reasons.
GauBlue Ngui: A ceramics specialist and sometime smuggler. He is described as impatient, sharp-witted, and a quick responder in dangerous situations. He is part of the core team with Hele. He was recently dismissed from a previous job but quickly hired by Banqo Systems. He has a past involving violence and is set to travel into orbit on Argo-Seven. He has a complicated relationship with "Hey" (Hele).
Dr t'Nguir (The Project Pilot): A key figure in the team, possibly a leader or highly experienced operative. She is direct, strategic, and tests loyalties. She was involved in the "La Portesse Battle" and vanished for months before returning. She is the individual GauBlue Ngui is implied to be thinking of when considering his "brief, dark heaven" in the cryobox. Also referred to as "the project pilot." Kin to Hele.
Research Coordinator Gojo Hei (but *never* refered to as such... aka "Under-fed Guard" aka "Lanky Quartermaster" and about half a dozen other aliases he specializes in, but "Research Coordinator" is probably the most accurate): He could turn up anywhere. He could be here for any reason, as far as you know. But, he's definitely there for a reason. Hired for his role, logs crate dimensions, makes jokes. You probably just think he's an ordinary underfed guard? That lanky guy on the docks? You'd be wrong. But most are.
Lanky Quartermaster: Attempts to intercept the courier delivering the MVA packet.
Partner/Companion to Hele: Accompanies Hele and GauBlue Ngui on missions and has an intimate relationship with Hele. Expresses disinterest in the "elite lifestyle" of the Foundation.
Sor Ngui: Briefly mentioned, asks to go to "your place." Hele's... *other*. And her connection into the Ngui. He won't show up often, he won't speak up when he does. But when you need an excuse--he's your excuse.
Dr. Liu t'Nguir: A chemist and expert in paramagnetic reactions, explaining the T-wave flattening. She is a high-ranking figure at Banqo Systems (the t'Nguir front-megacorp for mega-other-things you won't hear about on the streets).
Old Caretaker: One of two caretakers at the Riverside Hotel, stooped with age, provides local exposition. Refers to "Pa."
Young Caretaker: The second caretaker at the Riverside Hotel, barely twenty, provides local exposition and shrugs off questions about "Pa's" cabin.
Midland Viration Authority (MVA) Contact: A stone-faced individual who meets the team at the club and directs them to the underground spiral. Represents the MVA.
The Iceman: A man who stacks ice blocks onto a Model A outside the Riverside Hotel, indicating regular trade and the setting.
t'Nguir Leader: The leader of the tattooed punks from the KoHeak crime ring, a low side-branch of the t'Nguir, mostly optioned out of Banqo, former Banqo contact for GauBlue, who brags about GauBlue Ngui's past actions; mostly just to annoy the rest of the t'Nguir.
Biometrics Drone: An automated system that expresses concern over GauBlue Ngui's deteriorating health during his night shifts.
Gwiphe-Technician: A grinning technician at Banqo Systems who mutters "Baa-ngui-ngui."
The Roques: Dissident colonists living beneath Luna's mare basalt, plotting to retake the buried empire their t'Nguir ancestors ruled 150 years ago.
The Smuggler: Hiding in an abandoned riverside shop, preserving brains in achelic cylinders. He is killed by GauBlue Ngui.
The Gunman: Attempts to attack the team in the smuggler's shop and is killed by GauBlue Ngui.
The Messenger: A courier who delivers a packet from the MVA and slips past the Lanky Quartermaster.
