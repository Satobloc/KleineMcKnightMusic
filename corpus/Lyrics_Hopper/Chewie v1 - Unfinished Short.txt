An Unfinished Bezoar
	
By Nathan McKnight




[Mention that Stig was smoked out of his former home, and got a glance at Cindy's face as his toad was being shoved into it.]


	Stig awoke, ashamed, when the revelation announced itself. He had been dreaming of sexy little beard-nixies, but a quake of extraordinary magnitude jarred him, and he yipped a feeble "Oh my, oh my!" [His beard is his pride.]
	"Mistress Cindy, please forgive my dreams," went his familiar morning whisper, "for your slightest whim, I would gladly give my--" but he was cut off by another tremor.
He rubbed the sleep out of his eyes, troubled by a click-clickity-click echoing down from the grotto. 
With a yawn, he rose to see what the trouble was, padding through the soft corridor, sidestepping piles of moosh, grumbling and mumbling the whole short way. He ought to clear that up before he slips and breaks his neck.
He climbed past the dripping tail of the hairball, twitching his nose at its embarrassing unfinishedness, and surfaced in the Reflecting Pool. Sweet bile lapped at the central Bezoar, his hand-woven monument to beloved Miss Cindy.
There was a strange glint on the waves.
The grotto's usual lovely darkness was awash in bright preternatural light shining from above, filtering through the crenellated dome ceiling. He held up his hands and though his eyes were closed, he could clearly see the bones of his fingers.
"Hallelujah," he breathed. 
Then he shouted it over and over, waving his hands in the humid air. "Hallelujah! Praise be!"
The medallion in his front pocket began to warm against his chest. He clutched it tight, its dull edges shimmering with holy pain. Dearest Miss Cindy!
Click, the light went off. 
	Stig splashed to his knees next to the body of his liegess's Bezoar.
	"O Miss Cindy! I have seen your magnificent light! Your revelation shan't go unheeded. This is a great day in the Reflecting Poo..." he whispered over the quiet drip-dripping "...in the Pool of Light!" 
	Stig spun round, shooting glances all about. Surely, there had been a hairfall. 
	He ran his hands through the slippery liquid, but to his disappointment, all he found were the same pitiful wisps he'd passed over as unworthy during his last harvest. It had been weeks since there was a bona fide rain of angel hair.
	"Please," he wailed, "help me understand your wishes!"
	Maybe, he thought, just maybe, these few golden strands would be enough. He cursed himself for losing faith. Yesyes, that was it! A miracle! 
	With great reverence, he made an effort at weaving the hairs into the Bezoar. But far from miraculous, all he'd succeeded in weaving was a sickly straggle, marring the delicate curlicued surface. 
	Stig despaired.
"That won't do," muttered Stig into his beard, "No-no-n-li-no-no-NO," he pouted, "that simply won't do at all."
	"Dearest Cindy," Stig shouted, "what ever shall I do?"
	He bowed and mumbled, and tore at his beard. He threw his body onto the great Bezoar he had so lovingly woven, lo these long years, from its first spindly fibers. 
	"Why?" he pled, "Why am I so unworthy? Did I, in my hubris of desiring to create the Greatest of Great Bezoars...did hold out too long? One last rain of your Angelic fleece would be quite enough, I think, to adorn your wondrous Bezoar with a proper mantle. Please, please, send down your goddessly locks! Or if I, being so undeserving a subject have angered you, please, please my Liegess...give me a sign!"
	He folded whispered chants into ancient Gnomish tongues, once, twice, thrice. 

	And as he began a third round of chanting, a gargantuan blade sliced a gash through the dome, narrowly missing Stig's nose, but slicing off a silvery lock of his hair. 
	Reflexively, he snatched the hair, and backed into a stube at the end of the Grotto. Bracing cold air flooded in from the gape above. Stig couldn't take his eyes off the precious Bezoar, shimmering for this first time in harsh outside light. 
	He was ashamed at his creation. Its ugliness was astonishing.
	More astonishing still, through the hole, reached a giant set of fingers, their huge, ugly knuckle hairs visible through thin gloves. 
	Stig shuddered.
	The fingers plunged, obscene, into the grotto, penetrating his holy heart of worship, and clamped down--tightly--terribly--onto the Bezoar. 
	And they tugged. 
	All at once, Stig forgot his fear. He threw himself forward and dug his weaver-thick fingers into the fuzzy-wet surface of the Bezoar. 
	But the giant was too strong. The hairball was torn from Stig's grip, leaving clots of braided angel hair in his fists.
	The Bezoar was dislodged from its esteemed placement across the Pyloric threshold. Stig grasped his breast in disbelief, not bothering to wipe away the droplets splashed upon his face by the bile sluicing from the Bezoar. 
	He clenched his fists with righteous anger, boiling from his spleen.
	"It's....not.....FINIIIIIIISHED!" he screamed, leaping onto his Mistress's holy shrine. Cold air stung his wet face as he was wrenched, along with the Bezoar, from the warm, life-giving bile he'd called home. 
The giant's shiny hand slung Stig and the Bezoar carelessly into a chill metal basin. Stig's head thwacked against metal, and he passed out.

*   *   *

He woke some moments later, his teeth aclatter.
He stood and jumped to see out of the basin, landing with a tink. 	
The first jump he saw the giant, dressed in periwinkle robe and a mask over its muzzle. On the second jump, he saw at least two other giants abreast a long table. And on the third, a hint of golden hair. That gave him the strength to do what he did next.

Wincing at the sacrilege, Stig stood atop the Bezoar and gazed down at the breathtaking face of his sleeping Miss Cindy. 
Stig was alarmed that his liegess lie helpless under the knife of this horrid giant and a gaggle of hench-colossi. Most horrifying of all, a giant tray of torture implements left no doubt as to their intentions. 
The effort of the jumping and the climbing had warmed him, but his hands were chilled to the bone and he reached for the medallion, hoping it might be hot again. In so-doing, Stig's trembling hands knocked the lock of his own hair out of his chest pocket. Stig cursed a gnomish curse, and bent to pick it up, his mind incised with the image of the blade that cut it. 
That infernal giant destroyed everything!
Stig despaired. 
He heard a clank, then chanced another look over the edge of the metal basin. The giant had discarded the bloody knife in favor of an enormous curved needle trailing the most finely-woven rope Stig had ever seen. 
Stig ducked and huddled next to the bezoar, biting his nails until he tore them to the quick, unfeeling of the pain, then tearing at his beard until he pulled out clods of hair. He shuddered as the cold seeped into his core.
He wept into his fists, swaddling his vision in scarlet clouds. Through he haze, he saw the loops of his silver hair encircling bloody fingers. And suddenly, he cursed his stupidity. 
Of course! In his vanity, he had never even thought of it! The Bezoar would never be finished without proving his worth as its creator. In all these long years, his hands might well have been idle. Not once in its construction had he made a meaningful personal sacrifice. 
He grasped the medallion for strength, but recoiled.
What impotence! No! Now is the time for action, not apotropaic. 
The medallion had been a distraction. He pulled it out of his chest pocket, and tossed it clanking onto the floor of the basin. It gave a plaintive spark as he kicked it away, but he steeled himself. He'd no use for idols anymore. Not when his Miss Cindy lay infirm below, not when the giants had succeeded in stealing the unfinished Bezoar. 
 He resolved to return the Bezoar to its rightful owner somehow. 
He wrapped the curly tail in his hand, the silver hairs against the gold were breathtaking, and he swung himself over the edge of the basin, landing on tight-woven blue fabric. Assuming he'd have to repair it from the damage of the giant's grip anyway, he gave the tail a good hard tug. With a plop, it landed next to him. 
His chest heaving, he stared down the gap separating him from beloved Miss Cindy. A pocket of her gown hung open over the edge of the great table. That's where he would have to take the Bezoar.
He toed the edge of the blue cloth, imagining hurtling himself onto her thigh. He hesitated with fear. 
No--not fear. Something else: Uncertainty.
Why bother return it, unfinished? He was a disgrace. He should just admit failure. Maybe--maybe--the giants were right to take the cursed unworthy thing from Miss Cindy. His liegess would be happier rid of pitiful Stig. 
"No!" he shrieked. 
He had to return it, at any cost. Finished or unfinished. Now, he stood in fear and fear alone, ready to jump. 
He looked left, then right, and a glint of silver caught his eye from the giant tray of torture implements. He shook his head, stroked his unkempt beard.
And he had an idea.
Stig dragged the Bezoar to the other edge, and looked down at the giant shears and blades. He shivered. This table was much closer than the one Miss Cindy was on, but it was still a long ways. Carefully, he lowered the Bezoar by its tail. He followed it with his feet. His legs stuck out over the blades, but he kept going until he was bent at the waist, only his torso still on the table. The Bezoar dangled, and Stig grabbed a handful of blue fabric with his free hand, shimmying over the edge, dragging the fabric, and along with it, the big metal basin after him. He dangled one-handed above the torture implements, then let himself drop.
He held his breath, and let go.

*   *   *

The Bezoar cushioned his fall, but his landing flung a huge set of pincers off the tray onto the floor. This drew the attention of one of the giants, and he had to scramble into another dark blue cloth to hide. The cloth wrapped a curved set of shears with a toothed ratchet on the handle. Stig held his breath until the giant's hands had passed over, then he crawled out of the rolled fabric, leaving the Bezoar behind.
An array of unbelievably sharp blades lay before him, and he selected the nearest one. His gnomish ingenuity allowed him to puzzle out the clipping mechanism that held the blade to the gigantic metal haft. 
	He stole away under the giant rag again, crossed himself, and started shaving his beard with the arm-length blade. 
	
*   *   *

His beard hair would surely be enough, but when he'd finished with his beard, he shook off his cap and shaved the hair from his head. He would always go the extra yard for Cindy.
He ran a hand over his stubbly pate, proud of his sacrifice. He couldn't quite bring himself to rub his chin though. 
	With a loop wrapped around each finger, and a thumb tucking the loose ends under the golden fleece of the Bezoar, Stig wove in the ancient gnomish style. As fast as he could, and with such precision he couldn't believe his own fingers, he adorned the bezoar with endlessly intricate fripps and fandangles. 
	When he'd finished, the whole Bezoar looked like a sparkling curio egg. Never had such magnificent splendor emerged from gnomish hands. Its golden blobbiness scintillated with silver scrollwork of his own hair.
	Stig grasped his chest, a thrill dancing across his chilly skin.
	He screamed at the top of his lungs, "MISS CINDY, I SHALL DO RIGHT BY YOU MY GODDESS!"
	Suddenly, the thick blue cloth was whipped off of him, knocking him to his knees. One of the giants shrieked, but Stig kept his head. He wrapped the Tail o' the Bezoar round his forearm, heaved it off the torture tray, and wrenched his legs against the edge, sailing weightless across the gap.
	His arm snagged the pocket of Cindy's thin gown, and the full weight of the Bezoar yanked his other arm out of its socket. Stig screamed, but he grit his teeth. He was so close now.
	Before the giants got to him, he hefted the Bezoar safely into the pocket. 
	Relieved and dazed, Stig began to pull himself in behind it, but when he saw a giant hand coming to swat, he knew he had to draw attention away from the Great Bezoar.
	He rubbed his stubbly chin against his distended shoulder, and smiled.
	Then he let go.

*   *   *

His back broken, and cold creeping into his bones, Stig closed his eyes and let go.
