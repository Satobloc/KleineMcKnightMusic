The greater part of its plumage was of an **intense cinnabar red, with a gloss as of spun glass**, while springing from each side of the breast were little tufts of greyish feathers terminated by a **broad band of intense emerald green**. Adorning this little bird were two middle tail feathers modified into very slender wirelike shafts, each bearing at the extremity a web coiled into a **perfect spiral disc of emerald green**. The beauty and brilliancy of this insect are indescribable, and on taking it out of my net and opening the glorious wings, **my heart began to beat violently, the blood rushed to my head, and I felt much more like fainting than I have done when in apprehension of immediate death**. I gazed upwards and saw a large mass of something overhead which resolved itself into a **large snake, compactly coiled up in a kind of knot; I had slept soundly all night directly under him**. His face and a terrible wound in his head were a solid mass of flies, and thousands of insects were crawling over the blood clots on the stones beside him; it did not seem possible that a human being could live under such conditions, and yet the bystanders told us that **he had been lying there for thirty hours**. In the hospital compound we met a chair in which was lying an old man groaning loudly and dripping with blood; his poor wife was crying quietly and, between her sobs, was **offering the wounded man mustard pickles from a small dish in her hand**, believing food would cure all ills. One dog was ripped wide open, another received a horn-thrust in the side, and the **big red leader was thrown over a cliff to the rocks below**. Hotenfa carried him in his arms and laid him gently on a blanket in the temple, but the **splendid animal died during the night**. The bee-hunter rapidly got up—thirty, forty, fifty feet above the ground—climbing with as much coolness and apparent certainty as if he were going up a ladder, until he **drew his knife and cut off the comb at one slice close to the tree**. Caldwell and I were on our knees over the trap when suddenly a **low rumbling growl came from the grass not twenty feet away**. Suddenly at the left and just below us there came the faintest crunching sound as a loose stone shifted under a heavy weight, but **the blue tiger had slipped back into the heart of the lair**. He seemed the **very spirit of the mountains, a thing born of peaks and crags, vibrant with the breath of the clouds**. Should civilized man ever reach these distant lands, we may be sure that he will so disturb the nicely-balanced relations of organic and inorganic nature as to cause the **disappearance, and finally the extinction, of these very beings**. Our vast manufacturing system, our gigantic commerce, our crowded towns and cities, support and **continually renew a mass of human misery and crime absolutely greater than has ever existed before**. We allow over a hundred thousand persons known to have no means of subsistence but by crime to remain at large and prey upon the community; this, in a country which boasts of its mechanical skill and scientific knowledge, I can but **term a state of social barbarism**. As Yvette turned from the window her eyes were wet with unshed tears, and a lump had risen in my throat; not all the pleasures of the city could make us wish to end the **wild, free life of the year gone by**. The spot was redolent with the atmosphere of the lazy East; the East which, like the fabled "Lorelei," weaves a **mystic spell about the wanderer whom she has loved and taken to her heart**.

Among the executed were **two children, brothers, whose smaller sibling wept and pleaded to be permitted to kneel beside his older brother to die**. Nearby lay a man whose **head was partly severed from his body and who had been shot through and through, left to lie in the glaring sun from nine o'clock in the morning until dark suffering the agonies of crucifixion**. A tiger suddenly sprang upon a boy on a cart not half a mile from the village, **carrying him off into the jungle close by where his remains were later discovered as a few mangled bones**. A large Orangutan seized a man's arm and **made his teeth meet in the flesh above the elbow, which he tore and lacerated in a dreadful manner while the man remained powerless in his grip**. The cry of **"Amok! Amok!" resounds through the streets as a man with a bloody kris in his hand runs forward, stabbing at everyone he meets—men, women, and children—until he dies overwhelmed by numbers**. We were seized by one of those **treacherous currents and drifted out to sea, losing our last chance of reaching land while we had not a day's water on board**. Six men arrived at the village almost starved, having **escaped two praus where fourteen of their crew had been murdered by the bloodthirsty tribes of New Guinea**. A vine to which I had been clinging stripped off and I **began to slide toward a seventy-foot precipice, saved only when my rifle caught on a tiny ragged ledge and held me flattened against the cliff**. Serious infidelity is punished by **tying the woman and her paramour back to back and throwing them into the sea, where large crocodiles are always on the watch to devour the bodies**. The house began **shaking and rocking visibly, creaking and cracking as if it would fall to pieces while the inhabitants rushed into the streets screaming in terror**.

"There's a **whisper on the night-wind, there's a star agleam to guide us, And the Wild is calling, calling ... let us go**." The hills are **bathed in pink and lavender and purple; a more beautiful spot can hardly be imagined**. The **jagged peaks were crowned with gold where the morning sun had kissed their summits**, while fleecy clouds formed and floated upward to weave in and out of the vast snow craters. The sun **flooded the flower-filled courtyard, throwing bars of golden light through the twisted branches of a bent old pine**, as the **stars shone so low that they seemed like tiny lanterns in the sky**. The sea was transparent as crystal, and **tinged the rock-strewn slope which plunged steeply into its unfathomable depths with colours varying from emerald to lapis-lazuli**. The **bottom was absolutely hidden by a continuous series of corals, sponges, actiniae, and other marine productions of magnificent dimensions, varied forms, and brilliant colours**; it was a sight to gaze at for hours. We were living in a **great white palace, with ceiling and walls of filmy glittering webs converted into threads of crystal, forming a filigree lacework, infinitely beautiful**. The **slanting rays of the sun drew long golden paths into the mysterious depths of the mist-filled valley**, where every trunk was **clothed in a smothering mass of vines and ferns and parasitic plants, and thousands of ropelike creepers swayed back and forth with every breath of wind**. We beheld a **bright gem shining out amid the silent gloom of a dark and tangled forest**. The cocoa-nut trees were **brilliantly illuminated against a dark sky, appearing like a fairy palace supported on a hundred columns, and groined over with leafy arches**. The **ragged peaks turned crimson in the afterglow of the sun, and changed to purest silver at the touch of the white moonlight**.
