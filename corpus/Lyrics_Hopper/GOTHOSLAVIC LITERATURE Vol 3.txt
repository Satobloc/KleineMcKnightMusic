These passages are rendered in the formal Bogothic Standard Romanization (BSR), which utilizes Gothic lexicon and structure but incorporates the phonetic markers (like uu, x, and dd) associated with the Northern Frontier/Velarian tradition. Analytical hyphens are omitted to reflect the Standard Written Form of fused morphemes.
