# FROSCHLOCH

## Suno style prompt

Frighteningly opaque ethno-traditional industrial folk ritual; crooked 13/8 procession with sudden 4/4 victory-lap bursts; ancient Japanese woodblock atmosphere without pastiche; koto-like plucks, bowed bass, bassoon, low brass, taiko, dry Minnesota barn percussion, detuned music box, distant frog-throat choir. Intimate elderly Midwestern female lead, never shouted; children's counting voices in the bridge; wounded mass chorus singing WHY with Nancy-Kerrigan-like disbelief, increasingly harmonized and destabilized. Comic details remain deadpan. PRIME RULE: whenever the structure breaks, snap completely into the new structure. Final chorus loses the exit; end on a softly tested locked door, no resolution.
