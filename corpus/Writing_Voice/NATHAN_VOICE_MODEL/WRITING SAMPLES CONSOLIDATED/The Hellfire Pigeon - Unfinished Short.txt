Scene 1. HELLFIRE

"Hellfire!"
      That's what Pa had called it. I dropped the jar, shaking my finger in the air. It didn't hurt really; just a little sting. But it startled me enough to make me jump. I looked down. It wasn't broken, but I was dumbfounded for a second. 
      Then I picked up the glass-and-metal contraption from the ground and started rubbing my wool sweater on the rod that stuck through the thick cork. I?d nearly broken it, but this time when the snap! and the light pricked my finger, I didn't drop it. I was giddy. If Pa hadn't told me the story about finding the hellfire jar, I wouldn't know what it was, let alone what to do with it. 
      But this...well, this had potential.

      I remember having rolled my eyes. Father was always trying to get me into the things he loved as a child. Foxing, birding... But beagling was for farmers. 
      "No way, Pops," I had said, "I?m a city boy. I was made for horsing." 
      Father had laughed and I sat forward on the edge of my seat.
      "True that, true that," said father. "Well, anyway, them terriers, they all went down a hole. All down the same hole. That's how we knew something strange was going on.       "What was it?" I asked.
      "If I tell you," he said, "you have to keep it secret."
