# 03 — Nathan Taste Model

## Strong attractors

- Self-surprise; results that reveal an unanticipated but musically persuasive logic.
- Coherence without stylistic consensus.
- Concrete sonic bodies, objects, actions, rooms, media and mechanisms.
- Tight, underplayed, close vocal delivery; narrow or controlled projection.
- Bippy melodic cells without compulsory bubblegum, glossy pop or dance chassis.
- Rhythmic drag, off-bits, stagger, lurch, miscalculation and partial synchronization.
- Acoustic/traditional/regional materials retaining edges under digital or impossible processing.
- Glossolalia, phonetic hooks, unstable word-boundaries and semantic shimmer.
- Emotion functioning through strange structures rather than being replaced by strangeness.

## Productive pressures

- Casual/flat voice vs ornate or escalating arrangement.
- Strong invariant vs mutable implementation.
- Familiar hook vs alien body.
- Old media/tradition vs cheap sampler/glitch/machine execution.
- Existential or intimate text vs absurd vocal contagion.
- Local rhythmic cooperation vs unresolved harmonic/timbral friction.

## Common defaults to watch, not universal bans

- Cabaret/revue staging; audience-facing character entrances.
- Yelling, projection, belting or exclamation-driven escalation.
- Bubblegum brightness; glossy dance-pop/electronica as automatic solution.
- Generic mood-adjective atmosphere without causal sonic objects.
- Genre averaging that erases regional/acoustic accent.
- Clean optimization that makes the result more conventional.

## Guardrail

Never call conflict, malformed syntax, missing boundaries, nonsense or impossible instructions defects by default. First classify as: counterforce | ambiguity | overload | contamination | rupture | dead remnant | broken wiring.
