# 09 — Source Pools

## Preserve two representations

1. **Clean inventories** for deliberate selection, comparison and controlled recombination.
2. **Malformed/boundaryless fields** for false segmentation, accidental compounds, phonetic bleed and latent-space overload.

## Families

- Genres/traditions
- Meters/talas/groupings
- Instruments/techniques
- Vocal practices and vocal bodies
- Acoustic materials and spaces
- Recording media/transmission systems
- Social/performance situations
- Narrative/attention devices
- Production failures and defects
- Impossible acoustic objects
- Glossolalia/phonotactic material

## Recombination strategies

BLEND | DOMINANCE | CONTEST | TRANSLATION | OCCUPATION | INFECTION | ROLE_SWAP | GRAMMAR_TRANSFER | FALSE_RECONCILIATION | BOUNDARY_COLLAPSE | MALFORMED_AGGLUTINATION
