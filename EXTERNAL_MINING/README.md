# External Mining Quarry

This is the assistant-maintained external research and idea quarry for Suno, generative music, prompt behavior, and adjacent creative systems.

It is a source of ingredients for later work, not a specification for the user's creative process and not the future Workbench or Idea Bench.

## Relationship to the user's materials

- The user's existing hoppers remain the primary creative input and are not replaced, reorganized, or populated with web-mined material.
- This quarry contains externally discovered material: research, public creator observations, technical ideas, examples, alternative information structures, failures, and possible experiments.
- The future Workbench/Idea Bench is being developed in a separate thread. Nothing here should be treated as its architecture or as a requirement that the user's source material be normalized before use.
- Structured output is allowed to originate from unstructured source material. These records are aids to retrieval and recombination, not a demand that source material become tidy.

## Subfolders

- `raw_dumps/`: dated search/mining snapshots, short attributed excerpts, source observations, and contextual notes. Not a bulk mirror of third-party material.
- `structured_inputs/`: machine-readable source records, claim/evidence distinctions, and compact structured research inputs.
- `idea_mines/`: loose idea fragments, possible experiments, alternative prompt grammars, structural analogies, and creative affordances. Not a prescriptive taxonomy.

## Evidence labels

Distinguish:
- **Official documentation**: what the platform publicly says its product/features do.
- **Research result**: claims and methods in a paper or technical publication.
- **Creator observation**: an individual's reported experience or test.
- **Community theory**: an interpretation of model behavior, not verified internals.
- **Mining inference**: a possibility suggested by combining sources; explicitly speculative.

Do not infer Suno's private architecture, tokenization, weighting, or training process from surface behavior.

## Intake and rights

Keep canonical URLs, author/source, publication date when known, retrieval date, and confidence. Prefer short summaries and limited excerpts over reproducing source text. Respect platform terms, robots rules, rate limits, licenses, privacy, and attribution. Do not bulk-download audio or copy third-party datasets without authorization and rights review.
